正文 · 原文

Recognized for Completeness of Vision and Ability to Execute.

We are proud to share that Rapid7 has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Integrated Security Operations Center Systems. 

Our perspective: less stitching, more stopping threats

Most security teams don't struggle to find tools. They struggle to make them work together. We believe SOC analysts lose too much of their day moving between consoles and matching alerts to assets by hand, which is time not spent stopping threats.

To us, an integrated approach to security operations means detection, investigation and response working from the same data and the same view, so analysts can get from "what fired?" to "what do we do about it?" faster.

The importance of exposure management in the SOC

Rapid7 Incident Command, part of the Rapid7 Command Platform, brings together SIEM, SOAR, DFIR, attack surface management and threat intelligence. We built it on the idea that exposure management, and the context it brings, belongs inside the SOC, not next to it. In our view, when an alert arrives with what we know about the asset and how exposed it is, analysts can prioritize what matters instead of working from separate vulnerability reports and alert queues.

Context to act, not another console to check

We believe adopting an integrated SOC shouldn't be all-or-nothing. Incident Command is available in tiers from Essentials to Ultimate, so teams that want extra coverage can add managed services. That helps resource-pressured teams to start with what they need and grow at their own pace, spending less time building and maintaining integrations and more time on investigations.

To us, security operations work best when analysts have the context to act, not another console to check. We're proud to be recognized as a Leader in this Magic Quadrant, and we believe it reflects our focus on bringing detection, response and exposure context together for our customers.
