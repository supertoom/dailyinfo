Planning for 99.99 percent availability with the Amazon Cognito Plus feature plan

AI 导读

正文 · 原文

Amazon Cognito handles sign-in and access control for users, AI agents, and microservices with managed user directories, social and enterprise federation, and passwordless sign-in with passkeys or one-time passwords.

Sign-in is the front door to your application. If your customer identity and access management (CIAM) layer is unavailable, users can’t sign in or refresh their sessions, and they can’t reach the rest of your application. Your application’s availability directly depends on the availability of your identity layer.

Starting November 1, 2026, the Amazon Cognito Service Level Agreement (SLA) commits to a Monthly Uptime Percentage of 99.99 percent for user pools on the Plus feature plan, up from 99.9 percent. A user pool is a multi-tenant user directory that stores your users’ profiles and settings. User pools on Essentials and Lite feature plans remain at the 99.9 percent Service Commitment, while user pools already on Plus receive the new Service Commitment automatically.

This post explains why we made the change, how availability is measured, what’s in the updated SLA, how the SLA measures availability, historical availability data, and how to switch a user pool to Plus.

Why we raised the Service Commitment

Many customers use Amazon Cognito for sign-in to applications with strict availability requirements, such as patient portals and retail checkout flows. They need the services they build on to support high availability. We raised the Service Commitment for the Plus feature plan to 99.99 percent so that their identity layer is backed by an SLA that matches their commitments.

The new Service Commitment and Service Credits

For each AWS Region, during each monthly billing cycle, AWS commits to a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99 percent for user pools on Plus, and at least 99.9 percent for user pools on Essentials and Lite. If Amazon Cognito doesn’t meet the Service Commitment for your feature plan, you may be eligible for a Service Credit, as shown in Tables 1 and 2. For full terms and exclusions, see the Amazon Cognito SLA.

Table 1: Service Credit percentage for the Plus Feature Plan

Monthly Uptime Percentage Plus
Less than 99.99% but equal to or greater than 99.0% 10%
Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0% 25%
Less than 95.0% 100%

Table 2: Service Credit percentage for the Essentials and Lite Feature Plan

Monthly Uptime Percentage Essentials and Lite
Less than 99.9% but equal to or greater than 99.0% 10%
Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0% 25%
Less than 95.0% 100%

What 99.99 percent means in practice

The 99.99 percent Service Commitment is a 10-times improvement over 99.9 percent. In a 30-day month, the allowable monthly downtime drops from about 43 minutes to about 4.3 minutes. For teams that set availability targets for their own applications, this gives a stronger baseline to plan against and to share with stakeholders.

How Amazon Cognito measures availability

The SLA measures availability separately for each AWS Region. Amazon Cognito divides each monthly billing cycle into 5-minute intervals and calculates the percentage of requests in each interval that don’t return an HTTP 500 or 503 error. Intervals with no requests count as 100 percent available. The Monthly Uptime Percentage is the average of these interval values over the billing cycle, minus downtime covered by the exclusions in the SLA. For the full definitions and exclusions, see the Amazon Cognito SLA.

Historical availability over the past 12 months

As we introduce the new SLA, we also looked back at the past 12 months to show how Amazon Cognito performed against the new Service Commitment. Table 3 shows monthly availability for Amazon Cognito user pools from November 2025 through October 2026.

Availability was at or above 99.99 percent in 10 of the 12 months. It was below 99.99 percent in January (99.988 percent) and March (99.979 percent), but stayed above the previous 99.9 percent Service Commitment in both months and was at or above 99.99 percent the following month.

Switch to the Plus feature plan

If your user pools use the Plus feature plan, the 99.99 percent Service Commitment applies to them starting November 1, 2026, with no action needed. If your user pools use Essentials or Lite feature plans, you can switch them to Plus in the AWS Management Console for Amazon Cognito or programmatically. Before you switch, see user pool feature plans and Amazon Cognito pricing to compare features and costs. To learn more about the features that Plus adds, see the advanced security features with threat protection.

To switch a user pool to the Plus feature plan (AWS Management Console):

  1. Sign in to the Amazon Cognito console.
  2. Choose User pools and then choose your user pool.
  3. Choose Settings.
  4. Choose the Feature plans tab.
  5. Choose Switch to Plus.
  6. Review your choice and then choose Confirm.

Figure 1: Amazon Cognito console Feature plans tab with the Switch to Plus button

To switch a user pool to Plus feature plan (AWS CLI):

Important: The Update-user-pool command and the UpdateUserPool API operation reset any parameter that you don’t include in the request to its default value. To avoid unintended changes to your user pool, retrieve your existing configuration first and include it in your update request.

  1. Use the AWS Command Line Interface (AWS CLI) to retrieve your current user pool configuration with describe-user-pool: 
    aws cognito-idp describe-user-pool \
--user-pool-id <your-user-pool-id>
  2. Run the update-user-pool command, including your existing configuration parameters and the Plus tier: 
    aws cognito-idp update-user-pool \
--user-pool-id <your-user-pool-id> \
--user-pool-tier PLUS \
<your existing configuration parameters>

If you use an AWS SDK, set UserPoolTier to PLUS in an UpdateUserPool request, and include your existing settings in the same way.

Conclusion

In this post, we shared the updated Service Commitment for Amazon Cognito user pools on the Plus feature plan, how availability is measured, and 12 months of historical data. Starting November 1, 2026, user pools on Plus are covered by a 99.99 percent Monthly Uptime Percentage Service Commitment, with no action required if you already use Plus. We plan to keep investing in the availability and resilience of Amazon Cognito.

To review the full terms, see the Amazon Cognito SLA. To learn more about the features in each plan, see user pool feature plans and advanced security features with threat protection.
