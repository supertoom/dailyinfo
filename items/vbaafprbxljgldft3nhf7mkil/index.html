PRISM 2027 征稿通知 | 第二届攻击溯源与智能安全推理研讨会

PRISM 2027: Workshop on Attack Provenance, Reasoning, and Investigation for Security in the Monitored Environment（攻击溯源与智能安全推理研讨会）将于 2027年3月22日 在韩国首尔 Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas 酒店举行，与 NDSS 2027 同期举办。攻击溯源与智能安全推理（Attack Provenance, Reasoning, and Investigation）是近年来网络安全领域的前沿方向。本研讨会旨在探讨该领域面临的关键挑战，启发新的研究思路，并促进科研成果的落地与合作。我们接收正式论文（Regular paper），早期工作（Work-in-Progress Paper），系统演示与教程（Tutorial/Demo）。

我们关注的议题包括（但不限于）
• Provenance graphs and attack-path reconstruction
• Causal inference under partial or adversarial telemetry
• Graph learning, LLMs, and neuro-symbolic methods for detection and investigation
• Systems and infrastructure for provenance analytics (query, indexing, storage)
• Cross-domain and cross-layer correlation
• Investigation agents, analyst workflows, and human oversight
• Deployment in cloud, container, serverless, IoT, CPS, and OT environments
• Case studies in EDR, security operations, and incident response
• Log capture, integrity, compression, and reduction
• AI agent provenance across actions, tool calls, retrieval, and memory
• Investigation of prompt injection, tool abuse, and data exfiltration
• Real-time and cost-aware pipelines
• Explainability, uncertainty, and robustness to manipulated evidence
• Datasets, benchmarks, reproducible tooling, and evaluation methods
• Standards and interoperability (W3C PROV, OpenTelemetry, MITRE ATT&CK)
• Privacy, governance, and compliance for provenance data

研讨会组织委员会：
• 程序委员会联合主席：Wajih Ul Hassan (University of Virginia)、Pubali Datta (UMass Amherst)
• 组织联合主席：Zhou Li (UC Irvine)、Z. Berkay Celik (Purdue University)
• 工业界主席：François Labrèche (Sophos)
