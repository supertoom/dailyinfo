The Artex agentic AI penetration testing framework has become a common tool in Chinese threat actors’ arsenal, and Barracuda is one of its victims.

Published on GitHub in July 2026, Artex relies on a planner to formulate an objective and create tasks based on it, and on agents to perform those tasks, while the platform itself records the progress, findings, and assets.

It reportedly used DeepSeek v4.1-flash as the initial model, but can use other LLMs as well, and GLM-5.3 and Grok 4.6 were observed in certain instances. Overall, however, its users mainly rely on Chinese models, which are more permissive compared to US-made ones.

Artex was designed for security testing, but threat actors have been using it as part of in-the-wild intrusions since at least August, and its occurrence in real-world attacks has increased significantly during the past several weeks.

“The framework enables threat actors with minimal technical expertise to conduct complex, multi-target operations simultaneously, fundamentally accelerating attack velocity and expanding operational scale,” the FBI warns in a new alert (PDF).

Mentioning the framework’s use in attacks on South Korean financial firms and ConnectWise, the FBI warns of the risks Artex poses: faster attacks through automation, exploitation of neglected peripheral devices, and the ability to find and exploit supply chain attack vectors.

The framework’s developer removed their GitHub repository in October, but Artex continues to proliferate through other channels. In early November, Censys was seeing roughly 1,500 hosts running exposed Artex consoles.

In October, Artex was used by Chinese espionage group Vapor Panda in a campaign targeting over 200 organizations in 14 countries, including “foreign ministries, a European presidency, and US pharmaceutical, semiconductor, defense, and nuclear organizations,” SentinelOne reports.

Storm-0940 is another Chinese state-sponsored group using Artex, but evidence shows that financially motivated threat actors such as Storm-3358 have also adopted the framework, Cisco’s Talos notes.

Post-exploitation, the threat actors were seen abusing compromised credentials and keys, escalating privileges, deploying persistence, moving laterally, and stealing credentials and data.

“Observed impacts range from access to cloud secrets and mailbox data to the use of compromised applications to reach connected environments,” Talos says.

Amazon describes similar observations, noting that one of the most prominent Artex campaigns was focused on large-scale theft of credentials to access AI models, while another targeted cryptocurrency exchanges.

Barracuda, which fell victim to an Artex-powered intrusion, published similar findings: hackers used an exposed valid cloud access key to map its reach, then reused discovered secrets to access more systems and exfiltrate more credentials and secrets.

“In all, the attacker touched about 10 cloud accounts in roughly 44 hours. The work was methodical and tireless. The probe names, the other indicators we collected, the order in which the attacker worked through our systems, and its built-in routines for hunting credentials and wallets all match Artex,” Barracuda explains.

此次攻击发生于 11 月 1 日至 11 月 2 日之间，目标涉及密钥存储、配置、Kubernetes 服务账户和服务器元数据服务中的敏感材料，并在 Kubernetes 集群中留下了后门。

在一些服务器上，攻击者获得了 shell 访问权限，并利用该权限搜索 Ethereum 密钥库文件、种子短语文件、扩展私钥以及其他与钱包有关的信息。

Barracuda 表示，此次攻击与 10 月云应用托管平台 Railway 遭入侵事件类似。此外，它也符合此前报告的针对数十个 AI 开发者平台的一波入侵活动。

Barracuda 尚未对此次攻击进行归因，并指出 Artex 被许多团体使用。

Amazon 对两次由 Artex 驱动的攻击活动所作的分析显示，攻击者依赖的是早已为人所知的技术，但得益于 AI，其行动速度大幅提升。

Amazon 表示：“这些智能体在基础安全卫生措施缺失的情况下得逞，包括使用默认凭据或硬编码凭据、密钥遗留在代码和公开制品中、接口和推理端点未认证，以及软件未打补丁。确保这些基础措施到位，仍是最有效的防御手段。”

Miggo Security 联合创始人兼 CEO Daniel Shechter 表示：“所报告的 Artex 活动表明，自主攻击者并不需要零日漏洞。他们可以发现暴露的服务、利用授权缺陷，并在防御人员仍在分类处理警报和审批响应时持续调整攻击。当每一项防御行动都必须等待人员处理时，攻击者便掌控了节奏。防御人员需要从识别暴露面转向以机器速度实现经验证的防护。”
